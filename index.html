A Perfect Retreat for Scuba Diving Couples

Designed With Divers in Mind

Cozumel is internationally recognized for its spectacular coral reefs, clear waters, and exceptional scuba diving opportunities. It attracts divers from around the world who want to explore colorful marine life and experience some of the Caribbean's most impressive underwater environments.

Stingray Villa Cozumel is designed with these travelers in mind. We understand that scuba diving vacations require more than a comfortable bedroom. Divers appreciate practical facilities, convenient access to dive operators, and a peaceful place to recover after spending hours in the water.

Our property provides dedicated facilities for rinsing and drying diving equipment, helping guests manage their gear during their stay. We also offer fresh organic coffee from Chiapas, Mexico, making early mornings before dive boat departures a little more enjoyable.

Explore Cozumel's Underwater Beauty

From exploring beautiful coral formations to discovering tropical marine life, Cozumel offers unforgettable experiences beneath the Caribbean Sea. Popular diving areas around the island attract both experienced divers and visitors discovering scuba diving for the first time.

After a day of underwater exploration, guests can return to Stingray Villa to relax by the pool, enjoy the peaceful surroundings, and prepare for another exciting day on the island.

Our goal is to provide an accommodation experience that complements the diving lifestyle while remaining equally welcoming to couples who simply want to enjoy Cozumel.

Enjoy an Excellent Downtown Cozumel Location

Location is one of the most important considerations when choosing where to stay. Stingray Villa is situated in Centro, downtown Cozumel, providing convenient access to many of the island's popular places and everyday necessities.

The Caribbean Sea waterfront is approximately a five-minute walk away, while the San Miguel ferry terminal is around a fifteen-minute walk from the property. Guests can also explore numerous restaurants, cafes, local shops, and other downtown attractions without needing to rent a vehicle for every outing.

The surrounding neighborhood offers opportunities to experience the local character of Cozumel. Visitors can discover authentic Mexican food, browse artisan shops, enjoy oceanfront dining, and explore the lively atmosphere around Benito Juarez Park.

For travelers arriving from Playa del Carmen, the nearby ferry connection makes reaching the island straightforward. Our convenient location allows guests to spend less time arranging transportation and more time enjoying their vacation.
